Find your people.
Keep your herd close.

A short guide to the spaces, conversations, and places that make up Groupicorn.

01 / Start here

A private place for the people you keep choosing.

Groupicorn is a community app for learning, talking, and staying connected through the parts of life that are easier with other people around.

New here?Start in the web app, or download the mobile app if you are on a phone.Open Groupicorn ↗

02 / The app

One herd, smaller rooms when you need them.

Your herd is your home base. Subherds are smaller spaces organized around a shared chapter, interest, or conversation.

HerdsYour wider circle. A place to see what people are sharing and stay oriented.
SubherdsFocused spaces for a smaller group with something specific in common.
NeighsPosts and replies inside a subherd. Add a thought, ask a question, or check in.

03 / Conversations

Keep the useful conversations close.

Community chat is for the whole herd. Private chats are for the one-to-one and small-group conversations that do not belong in the wider room.

  • Use the community chat for shared updates and open conversation.
  • Use private chats when the circle should stay small.
  • Keep identifying or sensitive information out of public rooms.

04 / In person

Make room for meeting up.

Meetups help your herd turn an online connection into something concrete. Browse what is happening, see the route, and share the details with the people you want there.

05 / Platforms

Use the right screen for the moment.

The web app is designed for a larger screen and a longer catch-up. The mobile apps keep your herd close when you are away from your desk.
